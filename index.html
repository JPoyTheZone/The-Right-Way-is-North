The Right way is North is an action platformer set in a fantasy medieval world. Fight a myriad of Monsters, blast your way through the enemy stronghold or build your home and set out on an adventure to build renown. Set out on a quest to follow the Northern Star.

Meet the Heroes!

Ranger

Whittle enemies from afar as Ranger. Pepper them with arrows before they get near or slow them down as they approach.

Knight

Enter the fray with staggering blows as Knight. Land strikes that deal damage and scatter grouped enemies on a small area.

Prince

Zip into the battle as Prince. Enter as lightning and flash past enemy defenses, and eliminate key targets.

Paladin

Be graceful and versatile as Paladin. Enchant your weapon with stunning strikes or call upon heal or smiting light beams.
